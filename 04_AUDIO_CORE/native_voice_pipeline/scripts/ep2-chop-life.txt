Ẹ pẹ̀lẹ́ o, my dear people. Sisi Lola don land again, and today we dey talk food.

You see, when Naija person sit down for table, e no be ordinary thing. Na celebration. Na culture. Na love. Whether na jollof, na egusi, na ẹ̀bà and ẹ̀fọ́ riro, na pepper soup, food dey carry our spirit inside am.

My grandmother for Ìbàdàn, she used to say, "Ọmọ mi, oúnjẹ tí ó dùn jùlọ ní ó ní ìfẹ́ inú." The sweetest food na the one wey carry love. I never forget that one.

Naija food no be just chop. Na story. Each plate get history. The jollof rice wey we dey fight Ghana for, na grandmother recipe pass down. The suya wey hawker dey roast for night, na Hausa tradition. The akara wey we dey buy with pap for morning, that na Yoruba breakfast wey old pass me and you.

So when next you cook, no rush. Put your love inside the pot. Pepper am well well. Make e taste like Naija. And when you dey eat with family, laugh, gist, enjoy. Because that one na chop life proper.

Sisi Lola love una. Make una go cook something wey go shake the kitchen. Ẹ jẹ́ a chop life, ọ̀rẹ́ mi. Bye for now.
